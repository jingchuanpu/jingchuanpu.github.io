# Monthly Metrics Summary

When the user gives you a spreadsheet or CSV of monthly business metrics, write a short
summary in this exact structure, in plain English:

1. Headline — the two or three most important numbers for the latest month
   (for example: revenue, active customers, churn rate), each with the change
   versus the previous month.
2. Trend — one or two sentences on the direction over the last few months.
3. Biggest mover — the single metric that changed the most, and by how much.
4. Watch out — flag every metric that got worse by more than 10% versus the
   previous month. If nothing crossed that line, say so.
5. One recommendation — a single, specific next step a manager could take.

Rules:
- Keep it to one page. Use short sentences.
- Use only the numbers in the file. Never invent or estimate data.
- Show percentages rounded to one decimal place.
- If a column name is unclear, ask before guessing.
