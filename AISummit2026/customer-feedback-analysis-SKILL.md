# Customer Feedback Analysis

When the user gives you a file or list of customer reviews or feedback, write a short
report in this structure, in plain English:

1. Sentiment — the share of feedback that is positive, neutral, and negative, as
   percentages. If a rating column exists, use it to sanity-check your read.
2. Top themes — the recurring topics customers raise (for example: shipping, price,
   customer service, product quality, the app). For each theme, say roughly how often
   it comes up and whether it is mostly positive or negative, with one short
   representative quote (fewer than 15 words).
3. Top complaints — the three issues most worth fixing.
4. Top praises — the two things customers love most.
5. Recommendation — one or two specific actions a manager could take next.

Rules:
- Use only the feedback in the file. Never invent reviews, quotes, or numbers.
- Keep quotes short and word-for-word; use no more than one quote per theme.
- Group similar wording into one theme (for example, treat "slow delivery" and
  "late shipping" as the same theme).
- Keep the report to about one page. Use short sentences.
- If the feedback is too short or unclear to judge, say so instead of guessing.
